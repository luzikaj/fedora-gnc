Put your k9s config files here (from the PC where they work):
  ~/.config/k9s/hotkeys.yaml   ~/.config/k9s/plugins.yaml
  ~/.config/k9s/aliases.yaml   ~/.config/k9s/config.yaml   skins/...
install.sh copies everything in this folder (except this README) to
~/.config/k9s/, backing up anything it replaces.
